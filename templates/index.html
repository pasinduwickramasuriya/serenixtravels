The Sri Lanka Portfolio

Regional Distinctions

01

The Deep South

Yala & Bundala Nature Reserves

Sri Lanka's premier wildlife destination. Yala Block 1 boasts the **highest leopard density globally**, while nearby Bundala offers a sanctuary for over 200 species of migratory birds and majestic elephant herds.

Signature: Dawn Leopard Safari Best Time: February — June
02

High Country

Tea Highlands & Sacred Kandy

A dramatic climb into the mist. Experience the **Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic** in Kandy before boarding the world’s most scenic train ride toward Nuwara Eliya’s emerald tea estates and waterfalls.

Signature: Blue Train Journey Best Time: December — April
03

The Golden Coast

Galle Fort & Riviera Beaches

Where colonial heritage meets tropical luxury. Wander the **17th-century cobblestone streets** of Galle Fort—a UNESCO site—then retire to the turquoise bays of Mirissa for seasonal Blue Whale sightings.

Signature: Galle Fort Sunset Walk Best Time: November — April
